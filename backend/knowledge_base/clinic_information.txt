# MediVoice Family Practice
DEMO KNOWLEDGE BASE — FICTIONAL GP SURGERY

## Opening Hours
The practice is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:30 PM.
The practice is closed on Saturdays, Sundays, and UK bank holidays.

## Appointment Booking
Patients can request routine GP appointments by telephone or through the MediVoice AI receptionist.
Appointment availability depends on the practice schedule.
An appointment is confirmed only after the booking system successfully records it.
Patients should provide their full name, preferred date, preferred time, and reason for the appointment.

## Cancelling and Rescheduling
Patients can request appointment cancellations or rescheduling through MediVoice AI.
The system must verify the relevant appointment before making changes.
Patients should cancel as early as possible if they cannot attend.

## Repeat Prescriptions
Patients can request repeat prescriptions through the practice's normal prescription service.
Requests should be submitted at least two working days before medication runs out.
Prescription requests require review and approval by an authorised clinician.
MediVoice AI cannot prescribe medication or approve prescriptions.

## New Patient Registration
New patients can contact the reception team to ask about registration.
Registration requirements and eligibility must be confirmed by practice staff.

## Test Results
Patients should contact the practice for information about test results.
Only authorised healthcare professionals can interpret results and provide medical advice.
MediVoice AI cannot interpret clinical test results.

## Medical Emergencies
For a life-threatening medical emergency in the UK, call 999.
For urgent medical help that is not life-threatening, contact NHS 111.
MediVoice AI is an administrative assistant and does not provide diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or emergency medical assessment.

## Patient Privacy
Patients should only share information necessary for their administrative request.
Patient information must be handled according to applicable privacy and data protection requirements.
MediVoice AI must not disclose another patient's appointment or personal information.

## AI Receptionist Limitations
MediVoice AI can answer administrative questions using approved practice information.
MediVoice AI must not invent practice policies, opening hours, available appointment slots, or clinical advice.
If information is unavailable, the assistant should explain that it cannot confirm the answer and direct the patient to practice staff.
